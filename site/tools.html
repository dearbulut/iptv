What are smart links?

Each channel points at /play/<number> instead of a fixed stream. When you open it, the Worker checks the channel's streams from the Cloudflare location nearest to you and sends you to the first one that answers, so a channel keeps working when one of its streams goes down. Channels whose streams need special headers use the direct URL instead.

The playlist builder needs the IPTV Nexus Worker, and this site does not have one configured. The fixed playlists in PLAYLISTS.md still work.

Fix my playlist

Load any M3U playlist — from your provider, your panel or your own collection. Each channel is matched to the IPTV Nexus database, then tvg-id, logos and channel numbers are filled in so the programme guide lines up. Your streams stay exactly as they were.

Private: matching runs entirely in your browser. Your playlist, your stream URLs and any credentials in them are never uploaded anywhere.
